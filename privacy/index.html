Privacy

Your email, kept kindly.

The short version: we only use it for what you signed up for.

What we collect

When you join the list or reserve a place in The Godmother's Circle, we collect your email address. That's it.

What we use it for

To send you what you asked for — the free first chapter, the free Vision worksheet, Circle booking details — and occasional emails about the book, the Toolkit and the Circle. We never sell or share your details with anyone else for their marketing.

Unsubscribing and your rights

Every email has an unsubscribe link. You can also ask us at any time to see, correct or delete what we hold about you, using the contact details on the About page. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Who we are

The Wedding Godmother is run by Magali Dunkwu. Your data is processed on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
